The five pillars we assess

Organizations that get real value from AI are strong across five vendor-neutral principles. We score your team across all five, with detailed gap identification, clear explanations, and practical recommendations for what to do next.

01

Strategic intent, value & prioritization

Every AI initiative starts with a problem worth solving, a clear outcome, a baseline, and an accountable owner. If the problem is wrong, we reset it before evaluating options or making recommendations against it.

02

Sanctioned access & fast intake

Everyone who needs access can get it safely, and any newly identified use case is easy to flag, review, and handle.

03

Usage & cost visibility

AIOps measures provide full observability into usage and cost, down to per-task token utilization.

04

Enablement & shared context

Role-specific training, internal champions, shared prompt and playbook libraries, and one trusted source of company knowledge that people and AI both draw from.

05

AI inside core workflows

AI is built into the daily work, not bolted on in a separate tab.
