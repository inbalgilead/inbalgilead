Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Commitment

Inbal Gilead aims to make this website accessible and usable for as many people as possible, including people using assistive technologies.

Accessibility efforts

The website is designed with clear structure, readable text, descriptive links, responsive layouts, and alternative text for meaningful images where appropriate.

Known limitations

Some third-party embeds, external platforms, or downloaded files may not be fully controlled by this website. Efforts will be made to provide accessible alternatives or assistance where possible.

Feedback and assistance

If you experience an accessibility barrier on this site or need information in a different format, please contact Inbal Gilead and describe the page, issue, and preferred way to receive the information.

Contact

For accessibility feedback or support, contact inbalgilead@gmail.com.
